```text
Create a premium, high-fidelity luxury Indian e-commerce web application named "Ratnamayuri" (specializing in Luxury Jewellery & Silk Sarees) using a contemporary "Royal Peacock & Polished Platinum" design system. The visual layout must feel extremely expensive, sophisticated, and regal, completely avoiding any gold, yellow, beige, or antique-brown colors.

==================================================

==================================================
- Color Palette:
  * Primary Brand: Royal Peacock Blue (#0C2337) - deep, opulent navy-indigo base.
  * Secondary Brand: Imperial Emerald Green (#0B3B36) - rich, dark gemstone teal.
  * Accent Highlight: Royal Amethyst/Plum (#5B0C24) - used selectively for sale tags and discount highlights.
  * Background Light: Alabaster Silk (#FAF9F6) - warm, premium off-white textile background.
  * Background Dark: Obsidian Black (#0A0D10) - pure deep dark charcoal-black for banners and dark portals.
  * Line Borders: Satin Silver / Platinum (#DDE1E6 / #e5e7eb) - ultra-thin 1px minimalist boundaries.
- Typography:
  * Headings & Emphases: Playfair Display (bold & elegant light-italic) - classic high-contrast serif.
  * Display, UI Controls & Prices: Outfit - crisp, geometric, wide-tracked high-fashion sans-serif.
  * Body Text & Forms: Plus Jakarta Sans - clean, open-spaced readable sans-serif.
- UI Aesthetics: Borderless buttons, absolute minimal lines, glassmorphic floating widgets, spacious grids, 1px silver frames around images, responsive dark/light transitions, and clean micro-interactions.

==================================================

Sleek text navigation links: "New Arrivals", "Jewellery", "Silk Sarees", "Bridal". Right side has a minimalist search icon, a heart icon, and a cart bag with a circular indicator badge.

- Testimonials Carousel: Alabaster Silk cards with custom star ratings, deep emerald-green profile circle bubbles, and buyer locations (Jaipur, Bengaluru, Chennai).

==================================================
3. SCREEN 2: BROWSE CATALOG (LUXURY JEWELLERY CATEGORY)
==================================================
- Main Page Title: Centered, clean "Luxury Jewellery" header in Playfair Display serif, highlighted by a thin 1px horizontal signature separator.

  * A full-width white search bar bordered in silver, showing a search icon and a dark Peacock Blue "SEARCH" button.

  * Aspect ratio 3:4 product images in a thin silver border. Hovering scales the image slightly.

  * Solid Royal Peacock Blue bottom "ADD TO CART" button that slides in on hover for desktop, and is pinned statically for mobile.

==================================================

  * Left Column (Gallery): 4:5 vertical main image of "Aadhya Royal Emerald Choker" on a marble pedestal, with a floating Ruby Red "Heirloom Piece" tag. Below is a horizontal list of 4 square active thumbnail previews (detail shot, side view, hallmark stamp, premium velvet gift box presentation).

    - Signature signature divider line in Peacock Blue.

  * Trust Badges Bento: 2x2 grid of thin silver outlined boxes displaying minimal icons and text: "Lifetime Warranty", "Insured Delivery", "15 Day Returns", "Easy EMI".
  * Sticky Bottom Action Bar: Fixed footer container featuring a clean, white Outline button "ADD TO BAG" and a solid Imperial Emerald button "BUY NOW" side-by-side with high-contrast drop shadows.

- Metric Widget Cards (4 Columns): Four sleek cards displaying key business data: "Total Sales", "Active Orders", "Promoter Commissions", "Merchant Approvals" with custom Peacock-to-emerald gradients and bold, geometric numeric indicators.
- Area Chart: A smooth area-gradient visualizer (emerald-green line with fading gradient fill) tracking monthly growth on silver coordinates.
- Activity Database Table: Pristine table with thin silver boundaries showing details and custom pill-shaped status tags: "Approved" (green capsule) and "Pending Review" (silver-blue capsule).
```
