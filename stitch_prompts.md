# GOOGLE STITCH UI GENERATION PROMPTS: RATNAMAYURI
## Theme: Royal Peacock & Polished Platinum
## Purpose: Ready-to-use visual generation prompts for Google Stitch

> Copy and paste these high-fidelity prompts directly into the **Google Stitch** prompt bar or design canvas to generate premium, state-of-the-art UI pages for the **Ratnamayuri** redesign.

---

## 1. Master Style & Color System Prompt (Global Vibe)
*Use this prompt in Google Stitch's "Global Style Creator" or "Design System Generator" to establish the core visual identity:*

```text
Create a modern premium luxury Indian e-commerce design system named "Ratnamayuri". 
The style must feel extremely premium, regal, and contemporary, completely avoiding any gold, beige, or warm-brown colors.

Color Palette:
- Primary Brand Color: Royal Peacock Blue (#0C2337) - deep, opulent navy-indigo.
- Secondary Brand Color: Imperial Emerald Green (#0B3B36) - rich, dark gemstone teal-green.
- Accent Color: Royal Amethyst/Plum (#5B0C24) - used selectively for premium highlight markers, active statuses, and sale badges.
- Background Primary: Alabaster Silk (#FAF9F6) - soft, elegant off-white with textile texture vibes.
- Background Secondary: Obsidian Charcoal (#0A0D10) - deep charcoal-black used for absolute premium dark mode portals and solid banners.
- Borders & Dividers: Satin Silver / Polished Platinum (#DDE1E6 / #F2F4F7) - ultra-thin 1px minimalist boundaries.

Typography System:
- Serif (Headings): Playfair Display (semi-bold and light-italic) for royal, high-contrast, editorial headers.
- Display (Buttons/Prices/Trackers): Outfit - crisp, wide-tracked geometric sans-serif for premium modern branding.
- Sans (Body & Forms): Plus Jakarta Sans - clean, readable, contemporary sans-serif with excellent micro-spacing.

Design Vibe: Glassmorphic layers, crisp thin 1px silver border boundaries, spacious layouts, fluid micro-interactions, asymmetrical hero structures, and high-fashion Indian luxury editorial style.
```
